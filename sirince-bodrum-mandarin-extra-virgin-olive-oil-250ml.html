Origin & Story
📍 Bodrum & Şirince, Aegean Turkey

The Bodrum mandarin grows on Turkey's southwestern coast, where the Aegean meets the Mediterranean and the citrus is small, fragrant, and deeply aromatic. Cold-pressing these mandarins alongside Şirince olives creates something genuinely unusual — brightness and olive character in the same pour.

Light, distinctive, and beautifully suited to seafood, fresh salads, and any dish that benefits from a clean citrus finish.
